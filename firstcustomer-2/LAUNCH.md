# FirstCustomer — launch playbook

## Positioning

**One sentence:** Put a bounty on your next customer.

**What you sell:** A public, shareable referral campaign founders can launch in minutes.

**What FirstCustomer earns first:** $9 per launched bounty.

## Day-one offer

Keep pricing brutally simple:

- $9 to launch one bounty
- Founder chooses referral reward and goal
- Founder pays referral rewards directly
- No subscription on day one

## First 20 customers

Do not wait for SEO. Find founders already posting phrases like:

- “just launched”
- “looking for beta users”
- “need our first customers”
- “roast my landing page”
- “building in public”

Offer to build their first bounty with them.

## Suggested launch post

founders need customers.
everyone else needs money.

so I built a tiny market between them.

put a bounty on your next customer → FirstCustomer

## Direct outreach

Hey — saw you just launched [product]. I built a tiny tool that lets you put a public cash bounty on your next customers so people have a reason to actually distribute your launch. I can set up the first one with you.

## What to measure

- visitors → create form
- create form → $9 checkout
- paid bounty → at least one referral claimed
- referral links → product clicks
- product clicks → approved customer
- average promised reward
- founder repeat-launch rate

The most important early signal is not pageviews. It is whether founders who pay $9 actually receive attributable customer introductions.
